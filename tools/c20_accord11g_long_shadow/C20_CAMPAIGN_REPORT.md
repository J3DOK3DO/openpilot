## Admission and replay

`admission.py` validates exact 11G hybrid identity, route and trace binding, radar/camera/brake firmware, software and DBC SHA-256 fields, declared units, monotonic time, required signals with per-signal timestamps, lead validity and freshness, and overrides. The 2026-11-27 audit corrected its overclaim: a caller-supplied reference and digest cannot establish issuer or vehicle custody. The match status is now `REFERENCE_MATCH_UNVERIFIED` and replay mode `RECORDED_REFERENCE_MATCH_UNVERIFIED`; `MISMATCH` and `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE` remain fail-closed. A hash match is byte consistency, not independent vehicle verification.

No admitted authentic route, ECU firmware inventory, software/DBC provenance, or independent reference for this specific car was available in this worktree. A 2026-11-27 bounded host inventory found four older raw `rlog.zst` segments for route `0000009b--9f046e6157`, with hashes matching the historical extraction notes; exact-car identity remains unverified. The committed example manifest deliberately retains `UNVERIFIED` values. Only invented synthetic fixtures were replayed; no recorded Accord benchmark or vehicle improvement claim exists.

The replay compares two behavioral references on identical rows. It reports headway, acceleration-demand error, reference jerk, hold decisions, lead transitions, standstill, driver override, lead freshness, and reasons. It applies a numerical floor to observed braking in the synthetic reference and resets hold on lead-ID replacement. These calculations do not prove actual braking or prevent a runtime hazard because no runtime path exists.

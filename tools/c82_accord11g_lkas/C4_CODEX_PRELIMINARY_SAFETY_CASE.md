## CAN2 and controlsMismatch

`opendbc_repo/opendbc/car/honda/discovery.py:1-19,26-97` describes the C8.1 startup hypothesis: generic OBD multiplexing placed CAN2 on the OBD port, produced timeouts and an error/IRQ burst; the normal-bus route uses bus-0 identity requests and holds closed on uncertain identity. The recorded fixture description in `tests/test_accord11g_normal_discovery.py:1-5,122-135` reports about 35,000 protocol errors and a 17,862/s peak against the 16,000/s watchdog in an earlier 2026-11-12 route. This is a source-level explanation and offline fixture, **not** a current measurement of the physical cause. The selected C8.1 later route recorded no `interruptRateCan2` or `controlsMismatch` (`independent_review_20261114/C8_2_INDEPENDENT_REVIEW.md:31,95`). Do not infer that CAN2 hardware, OBD wiring, connector, firmware, or alternate startup conditions are healthy.

At host level, `selfdrived.py:514-520` adds `controlsMismatch` for a blocking Panda fault; `openpilot/starpilot/aol/panda_faults.py` and `opendbc_repo/opendbc/car/honda/tests/test_accord11g_c8_1_transport.py:383` treat `interruptRateCan2` as blocking. Native/host status loss also yields `controlsMismatch` (`selfdrived.py:2060-2065,2203-2211`). The Phase 2+3 lifecycle injects a fault and demonstrates simulated propagation and zero withdrawal; it cannot distinguish OBD mux, bus wiring, transceiver, ECU load or timing on the actual vehicle. Healthy CAN2 at startup and through transitions is a physical gate; no fault masking or speculative source fix is proposed.

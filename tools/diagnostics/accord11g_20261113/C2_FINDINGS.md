# Campaign 2 findings

## FDCAN2 startup fault

The 82 complete rlogs were decoded one segment at a time. Route 0 contains a raw Panda snapshot with FDCAN2 IT0 rate 17,862/s, above the 16,000/s watchdog. **Six raw snapshots in two integer monotonic seconds** report an above-limit last-one-second rate; these flags do not show two seconds of continuous excess. The active `interruptRateCan2` fault persists through 10,873/10,912 Panda samples in the prior full-resolution aggregate. The first active fault sample is at 19,575,839,471 ns and the first reported over-limit rate is at 19,676,046,137 ns, about 0.10 s later. The rate describes a preceding measurement window, so this ordering does not locate the triggering interrupt or prove when excess began. The fault then persists while the published rate falls. Exact raw Panda and first controlsMismatch timestamps are in `c2_timeline_summary.json`. Route 1 starts with the fault already active despite no sampled rate over limit; the trigger may precede that rlog. Route 2 has no active rate fault or mismatch. `faultTemp` is sticky and is not counted as active.

The original Campaign 2 processor calculated consecutive 10 Hz deltas but summed only the nearest-second rows. It therefore missed roughly nine of every ten observed intervals: the previously published route 0 error/RX sums **5,780/81,968**, route 1 **1,968/1,430**, and route 2 **3,150/290,833** are invalid. The corrected processor sums deltas across every consecutive raw Panda snapshot, excluding reset or nonmonotonic-time boundaries. The corrected total-error/RX/TX sums are route 0 **35,235/830,103/51**, route 1 **35,543/15,358/51**, and route 2 **35,437/2,831,533/51**. No counter epoch boundaries occurred in these decoded streams; independent first-to-last counter differences confirm the sums. These are observed counter changes over each available route, not all physical frames or comparable rates, and route durations differ greatly. Neither the error counters nor the sampled IRQ rate distinguish RX traffic from protocol-error interrupts. The exact electrical/configuration cause remains unknown. Init transitions include ELM327 safety param changes before Honda Bosch 80; their timing does not establish a mux cause.

The final route 2 segment 62 is labelled TRUNCATED. Its compressed bytes match the source manifest SHA-256, so exclusion is due to the original partial file rather than processor corruption. No wall-clock time is assigned: manifest segment start times are estimated and were not verified against an independent per-route monotonic anchor.

## AOL and secondary issues

The recorded CP alternative experience is 0 while the current marked independent path requires 32. The saved preference 1 cannot prove settings qualification, physical intent mapping, native safety receipt, current session/inventory or a granted lateral axis. Stock ACC plus lateral on route 2 follows the ordinary coupled path; see `HONDA_AOL_MATRIX.md`.

Route 2's single `commIssue` occurs at logMonoTime **27,428,289,831 ns**; the adjacent cloudlog entries at 27,429,020,456 and 27,429,094,570 ns report `not_alive=['carOutput']`, with no listed invalid or frequency failures. In a ±2 s window, decoded carState is valid with no CAN timeout, stock ACC disabled and speed near 31.8 m/s; sampled managerState shows card, selfdrived, ui, modeld and camerad running as expected. `carOutput` messages exist on both sides of the event. This supports a transient liveness/timing issue; it does not prove a process crash or CAN2 fault. UI FPS warnings count 25–32 FPS in prior evidence and appear in log classifications, but no synchronized camera/model liveness failure is established. u-blox NACK and almanac logs are separate GNSS conditions, with no demonstrated CAN2 causal link.

## Confidence limits

High confidence: source rate/fault mapping, observed active fault, marked-profile absence, exact carOutput liveness classification. Medium: temporal ordering at raw Panda/event timestamps. Low/unknown: physical FDCAN2 root cause, why the marker was absent, native session state, and any C6-12 vehicle outcome. No physical safety qualification follows from this offline analysis.
